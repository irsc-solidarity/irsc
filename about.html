Our Vision

A harmonious India and world where people of all faiths and beliefs are free to practice their own faiths without encroaching on others, live in mutual respect, understanding and shared commitment to peace, justice and human dignity.

Our Mission

Objective 01

Engage in meaningful dialogue between diverse faith communities and followers of diverse spiritual traditions to promote mutual understanding.

Objective 02

Protect cultural and religious diversity as a fundamental pillar of our collective identity.

Objective 03

Take a stand against extremism, absolutism and supremacist ideas in the name of religions, faiths and beliefs.

Objective 04

Promote spirituality and values of love, empathy, compassion, and solidarity, while advocating for simple living.

Objective 05

Collaborate with religious institutions and organizations to undertake socially relevant services for the needy.

Objective 06

Work towards building bridges across faith communities by addressing prejudicial attitudes and harmful stereotypes.

Final Goal

Promote peace, social justice and harmonious relations between faith communities in Mumbai.

Why We Exist

The Rationale

Given the present sociopolitical context—marked by increasing extremist expressions and assertions of religious supremacy—there is an urgent need to revive and amplify the ethical teachings of various religious traditions that emphasize coexistence and empathy.

Religion can be a source to guide human conscience towards compassion, forgiveness, truth, and nonviolence, acting as a bridge between different communities.

Background & Impact

The Inter-Religious Solidarity Council (IRSC) is a platform dedicated to fostering dialogue, understanding, and cooperation among diverse faith communities. Rooted in the principles of secularism, pluralism, and shared humanity, IRSC brings together religious leaders, scholars, and civil society actors to address contemporary social challenges through an interfaith lens.

Since its inception, IRSC has convened a wide range of initiatives—including interfaith dialogues, public forums, and cultural engagements—that encourage mutual respect and collective responsibility. Its work has included landmark gatherings such as the Mumbai Parliament of Religions , inspired by the enduring legacy of Swami Vivekananda, alongside ongoing collaborations that build bridges across communities.
